## 1. Diagnosis

The 5–10 largest sources of context entropy, largest first.

### 1.1 HANDOFF.md is three eras deep, and two dead eras still speak in the imperative

- **What it is.** The file header (`HANDOFF.md:3`) says *"Updated 2026-08-08.
  Branch `feature/typed-legs-routing`"* while the top section is dated
  2026-08-21 and the actual branch is `feature/geoapify-routing`. Below the two
  live findings sections (L17–511), roughly 60% of the file (≈950 of 1,717
  lines) is closed, resolved, or superseded content — and two superseded
  sections still carry standing instructions: the 2026-08-08 substrate section
  (L1149–1197, *"**Do not re-open or re-argue this.** MapLibre is retained…"*)
  and "Base map — MapLibre is the substrate" (L1551–1578). Both are contradicted
  by the 2026-08-15 ADR (`Docs/decisions.md:1347–1350`).
- **Evidence.** HANDOFF.md:3, 17, 1149–1197, 1336, 1551–1578; decisions.md:1286,
  1347–1350. Line count: 1,717.
- **Cost.** HANDOFF.md is the designated delivery channel for PO findings
  (PO.md:391). A fresh session that reads it top to bottom receives a stale
  branch name, a stale PR #11 status, and two imperative "do not reopen"
  instructions defending a parked decision — the exact failure PO.md:71–75
  warns about. `Docs/eng-session-P4.md:28–30` already has to warn every
  engineering session that "the handoff is stale" — a per-session tax paid
  because the file itself does not say so.

### 1.2 PO.md's Routing lock is the "superseded lock" hazard its own text warns about

- **What it is.** PO.md:79–80 locks *"OSRM stays as the routing engine… The
  provider is not selected"*; PO.md:155 states current implementation is
  `OSRM`; PO.md:90 defers lifting the detour gate to "when the migration
  happens and not before". The provider was selected (Geoapify,
  decisions.md:1519–1521) and the migration is committed
  (`e366df2`, `556f828`, `1cedbd2` on this branch; confirmed by
  eng-session-P4-visual.md:12–13).
- **Evidence.** PO.md:77–93, 149–166; decisions.md:1519–1641. PO.md:71–75 is
  the file's own warning that a superseded lock "would have had a PO session
  defending a dead decision."
- **Cost.** PO.md is the charter every governance session loads. The Rendering
  lock was correctly amended on 2026-08-15; the Routing lock was not amended on
  2026-08-20, so the charter now enforces a dead decision one section after
  warning about exactly that.

### 1.3 CLAUDE.md's first paragraph is wrong on both facts it states

- **What it is.** CLAUDE.md:3 declares the authoritative spec "v1.7" (it is
  v1.8, spec:12, bumped 2026-08-20) and CLAUDE.md:5–6 declares the phase map
  "P3.5 = Replay MVP (current release target), P4 = Story Director" — both
  contradicted 120 lines later by CLAUDE.md:127 ("Current phase: 4 — films
  worth keeping. Phase 3.5 CLOSED 2026-08-15") and by the 2026-08-15 closing
  ADR.
- **Evidence.** CLAUDE.md:3–9 vs CLAUDE.md:127–133; kamome-poc-spec.md:12;
  decisions.md:1251–1283.
- **Cost.** This is the always-loaded working memory; its opening paragraph is
  the first thing every session reads and it primes the wrong phase, the wrong
  Phase-4 content, and the wrong spec version before the correction arrives.

### 1.4 §0's absolute privacy claim no longer matches decided policy

- **What it is.** CLAUDE.md:21–25 states location history is "**never** …
  transmitted" with a "single exception" of explicit user-initiated share. Since
  2026-08-16 (accepted) and 2026-08-20 (c) (confirmed and refined), routing
  sends real trip coordinates to Geoapify on every photo-import — walks
  included — and recorded traces are decided as sent for map-matching
  (decisions.md:1490–1496, 1693–1699, 1758–1762), with a narrowing lean for
  recorded trips parked to Capture Beta (decisions.md:1883–1894). CLAUDE.md
  acknowledges the cost in passing at L229–230 but §0 itself was never amended.
- **Evidence.** CLAUDE.md:15–43 vs decisions.md:1490–1496, 1686–1729,
  1731–1796.
- **Cost.** §0 is the project's highest-authority standing principle and is
  cited by name across the repo (HANDOFF, pre-launch, session prompts). A
  reader who takes §0 at its word will block or mis-review routing work; a
  reader who knows the ADRs must hold the correction in their head every time
  §0 is quoted. The privacy notice work (pre-launch item 7) will be drafted
  against this text.

  re-deriving; the no-magic-numbers rule puts truth in config, but the docs

## 5. Proposed authority map

Adapted to Kamome's actual paths. When two levels disagree, the higher wins; the
newest entry wins within a level (PO.md authority hierarchy, Arch.md §0).

- **Product intent** → `Docs/kamome-poc-spec.md` (vision, product rules,
  §0-of-spec Rules of Engagement, phase *definitions*) — after its §7 status
  column is corrected, it is the spec of what Kamome is, never of what is
  currently happening.
- **Current state** → `CLAUDE.md` ("Current phase" section + standing
  principles + procedures) for every session's first load; `HANDOFF.md` for
  session-grained live findings and open experiments only. Both files stop
  carrying closed history.
- **Architecture decisions** → `Docs/decisions.md`, append-only, the single
  ledger. Supersession happens by a newer dated entry that names what it
  amends (the 2026-08-15 → 2026-08-08 chain is the model). No other file may
  hold the only copy of a decision.
- **Engineering rules** → `Arch.md` (session conduct) and `PO.md` (governance
  charter, locked-decision register — kept current per its own L71-75 rule).
- **Task-specific docs** → `Docs/camera-arcs.md`, `Docs/cross-region-journeys.md`,
  `Docs/vector-tile-pipeline.md` (dormant), `Docs/pre-launch.md`,
  `Docs/eng-session-*.md` — each with an explicit status line (decided /
  recommended / dormant / spent) as camera-arcs.md and cross-region-journeys.md
  already model.
- **Historical records** → `Docs/decisions.md` entries (never rewritten),
  `Docs/demos/*`, `Docs/device-test-*.md`, `Docs/prototype/`, and a new
  `Docs/_archive/` for closed HANDOFF/CLAUDE sections — moved intact with
  their dates, never deleted, never loaded by default.
