## What works

- **One day on screen at a time.** The pill's old day fades (0.25 s), the slot stays empty under the
  stamp, and the stamp lands as the pill's own type: `RecapDayStampRenderTests` measures the landed
  frame against the plain HUD and finds no visible seam.
- **It is Kamome's, not Polarsteps'.** No card, no date banner: a pressed ink mark with the wordmark's
  gull in its border, askew, with gaps where the ink did not take, which then becomes the HUD. Each day's
  border wobbles differently (FNV-1a of the day).
- **No film time.** Every beat is where it was (`testTheStampAddsNoFilmTime`). Measured on the real trips,
  with the stamp starting once the previous stop's photographs close: every turn on both trips fits.
- **Never over a photograph or a card**, tested frame by frame on a synthetic trip.

## For Chiu — the three choices

1. **Where it presses** (`stills/position-compare.png`). *Under the pill* (first cut): it lands a short way
   up, and only ever covers map. *Upper centre*: reads more like a chapter heading, but on the road that
   band holds the trail; on Iceland's first turn it sits on the trail beside the arriving town's name.
2. **The ink** (`stills/ink-compare.png`). Vermilion `#D9452E` (first cut) or the film's accent `#FF6A3D`.
   ⚠️ The grade and the vignette go over all chrome, and the vignette is darkest in the corner, so what the
   film shows is darker than either value. Measured on that turn (median ground): vermilion under the pill
   2.0:1 dark, 2.7:1 light; in the upper centre 3.1:1 and 2.6:1; the accent 1.9:1 on light. No single ink
   clears 3:1 on both maps in the corner; it reads by hue. If it reads too dim on a phone, the levers are a
   brighter ink on dark only (one ink becomes two), or drawing the stamp above the atmosphere (it would no
   longer match the HUD it lands in). Neither is taken here.
3. **The timing.** 0.25 s clear, 0.35 s press, 0.9 s hold, 0.55 s land; the hold shortens where the room
   is short (to 0.25 s at `min_s` 1.4). INFERRED. Where the road in is short the stamp lands inside the
   arrival beat, up to 1.0 s after the car parks, so the HUD's day turns over that much later than it did.

## Recommendations

- Judge the corner stamp on the dark film on a phone first: it is the dimmest case measured.
- The empty slot in the pill (" · Town") for about 1.5 s reads as a slot waiting; if it reads as a bug, the
  pill could shrink to the place alone and grow back as the stamp lands.

## Polish

- The stamp is set in the HUD's Helvetica Neue Bold. A zh-Hant film's 「第 3 天」 falls back to the system's
  CJK face, as the HUD's does today (#202 covers the town names' version of this).
- The light map's distance readout ("548 km") is pale on pale terrain in these renders. That predates the
  stamp and is outside this PR.

## Kamome identity

Emotional axis, on purpose: the one hand-drawn thing in the film's chrome, a souvenir mark collected per
day, and it hands off to the structural HUD rather than living beside it. The type stays structural.

## Recommendation

Ship the first cut (under the pill, vermilion) for Chiu to judge from the films. Decide position and ink
from the side-by-side stills, and the dimness on a phone.
